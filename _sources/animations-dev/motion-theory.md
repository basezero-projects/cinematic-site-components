## Distilled Use

Use this as a taste filter, not as a module source. The useful takeaway is that good animation has purpose, timing discipline, interruption behavior, and restraint.

## Local Rules Reinforced

- Do not animate frequent actions unless the feedback is near-instant.
- Entry motion should feel responsive, not delayed.
- Springs are useful when gestures can be interrupted.
- UI should not animate from nothing.
- Reduced motion means fewer and gentler movements, not broken states.

## Why It Belongs In The Source Notes

The cinematic library needs strong section-level effects, but the site still has to feel usable. This source supports the quality gate and helps keep big animations from becoming junk.

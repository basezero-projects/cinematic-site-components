Module 40 - WebGL Material Reveal

Reveal the finish, not a filter.

Use this when the surface is the claim: coating, restoration, product finish, ingredient quality, material spec, or any before-and-after where a flat wipe would undersell the detail.

The shader should make inspection easier.

A useful reveal has two real layers, a controlled edge, and a final state that proves what changed.

Raw layer

Start with the untreated surface.

The first frame should make the baseline visible: grain, scratches, dullness, oxidation, unfinished material, or the part before restoration.

Why this belongs in the library.

Shader reveals are easy to misuse as noise. This one is useful because it compares two surface states in one inspectable object. It fits product materials, restorations, coatings, food detail, fabrication, and editorial feature images where the transition itself explains the value.

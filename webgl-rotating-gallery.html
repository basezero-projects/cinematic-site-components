WebGL rotating image gallery

Rotate the gallery as the page moves.

Useful for lookbooks, architecture, art, and premium portfolios where the gallery itself should feel spatial. The fallback is a plain image grid.

InputFive textured planes generated in canvas, uploaded as WebGL textures.
MotionScroll controls the ring rotation. The render loop eases toward the scroll position.
FallbackNo WebGL or reduced motion switches to static cards.

Module 38 - 3D Camera Tour

Direct the camera through the thing that matters.

Use this when depth is part of the proof: product construction, venue walkthroughs, architecture, hardware, gallery installations, or a case-study object that deserves more than a flat hero image.

A scroll scene should make inspection easier.

The camera moves with a purpose: wide read, material pass, detail pass, proof angle, then a resolved offer frame.

Opening read

Start wide enough to understand the object.

The first camera position should give the visitor the object, the surface, and the mood before asking them to inspect detail.

Why this belongs in the library.

Most 3D scroll demos move because they can. This module treats the camera like an editor. It is strongest when a visitor needs to understand an object, place, or build process through a few planned angles. If the page has no meaningful thing to inspect, use a lighter module instead.
